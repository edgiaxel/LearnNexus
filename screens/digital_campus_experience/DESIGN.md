## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a welcoming, collegiate digital commons tailored for independent scholars, collaborative study groups, and everyday campus life. The visual identity bridges the tactile warmth of physical campus stationery with the functional clarity of modern productivity interfaces.

### Personality & Tone
- **Atmosphere:** Calm, encouraging, studious, and unhurried.
- **Visual Stance:** Warm minimalism with tactile parchment cues—replacing stark, clinical utility with organic comfort.
- **Emotional Impact:** Reassuring, grounded focus; low cognitive fatigue; an inviting community space that feels academic without being rigid.

### Style Directives
- Avoid high-intensity saturation, neon accents, dark futuristic metaphors, or sharp industrial borders.
- Center layouts around expansive, breathing space, gentle contrast, and natural paper-like card containers.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in soft botanical hues, warm paper tones, and grounded charcoal typography to ensure natural warmth and accessibility across long study sessions.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#8FAE91`):** Muted Sage Green. Used for primary interactive actions, selected states, key indicators, and active navigational tabs.
- **Secondary (`#C9BDE0`):** Soft Wisteria Lavender. Applied for secondary highlights, tag badges, course classifications, and supportive illustrations.
- **Tertiary (`#EAC6AE`):** Warm Peach. Reserved for conversational notices, alerts, reminder badges, and community tags.
- **Neutral Core (`#343B36`):** Deep Forest Charcoal. The primary text color, delivering crisp, comfortable contrast against light parchment backgrounds without the harshness of pure black.

### Surface & Canvas Tone
- **App Canvas:** `#F7F4EC` (Warm Cream). Acts as the parchment base across all major layouts.
- **Elevated Surfaces & Cards:** `#FFFFFF` (Soft White). Creates soft distinction against the canvas.
- **Border / Structural Tone:** `#E3E5DC` (Muted Warm Stone). Delivers gentle structural definition without heavy division.
- **Secondary Text:** `#727B72` (Muted Slate Sage). Used for captions, metadata, timestamps, and placeholder copy.

### Auxiliary Accent Roles
- **Butter Yellow (`#F0DFA7`):** Academic bookmarking, highlight notes, and pending states.
- **Dusty Sky Blue (`#A7C5D4`):** Library materials, attachments, and informational indicators.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the warm, human geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and sections with the disciplined clarity of Inter for sustained body reading and data entry.

### Role Allocation
- **Headlines & Titles:** Rendered exclusively in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. The subtle organic shapes and approachable curves preserve an academic yet friendly tone.
- **Body & Data:** Rendered in **Inter**. Its optimized apertures and neutral proportions ensure maximum readability across syllabus notes, schedules, and assignment prompts.
- **Labels & Tags:** Typeset in medium weights of Inter to maintain scannability on small-scale UI elements such as status chips and calendar cells.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes structured clarity with calm negative space, avoiding cramped toolbars or cluttered dashboards.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum 32px (`2rem`) page margin. Max-width content boundaries remain capped at 1280px for standard views to protect line length.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px page margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) page margins.

### Spacing Cadence
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled visual items, such as icon-to-label offsets and chip padding.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, stack gaps, and list row separation.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for distinct module groupings within cards and form field blocks.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) to establish clear sectional boundaries across the campus view.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle tonal layering and soft ambient depth, avoiding heavy drop shadows and harsh outlines.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Base Canvas:** The warm cream (`#F7F4EC`) substrate forms the foundation.
- **Tonal Stacking:** White (`#FFFFFF`) containers resting on the cream background create the primary elevation layer without requiring shadows.
- **Ambient Shadow (Float):** When overlays or raised components are necessary (e.g., active dropdowns, modal study timers, popovers), apply an ultra-soft, warm-tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(52, 59, 54, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(52, 59, 54, 0.03)`
- **Structural Separation:** Use 1px low-contrast borders in `#E3E5DC` to subtly define cards, divider rules, and input perimeters rather than heavy shadows.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a friendly, rounded visual language that complements its paper and campus identity.

### Geometry Values
- **Base Radii (`roundedness: 2` / 8px):** Applied to standard interactive targets including text inputs, dropdown toggles, buttons, and utility controls.
- **Medium Radii (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Applied to content cards, study module blocks, assignment panels, and modal containers.
- **Large Radii (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Reserved for hero surface cards, calendar viewframes, and bottom sheets on mobile.
- **Pill Shape:** Retained exclusively for status badges, tags, and small filter chips to emphasize touchability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#8FAE91` with text and icons in `#FFFFFF`. Border-radius set to 8px. Hover state deepens slightly to `#7E9D80` with a smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Background in `#F7F4EC` with `#343B36` text and a subtle 1px border in `#E3E5DC`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#727B72` text, shifting to `#E3E5DC` at 30% opacity on hover.
- **Padding:** 10px vertical by 20px horizontal for default buttons; 6px vertical by 14px horizontal for compact actions.

### Cards & Panels
- **Background:** Crisp soft white (`#FFFFFF`) set over the `#F7F4EC` canvas.
- **Edge:** 1px continuous border using `#E3E5DC`.
- **Corners:** 16px (`rounded-lg`).
- **Internal Padding:** 24px (`space-lg`) on desktop; 16px (`space-md`) on mobile screens.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** White background, 1px `#E3E5DC` border, 8px radius. Text in `#343B36`, placeholder text in `#727B72`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border in `#8FAE91` complemented by a soft 3px outer ring in `rgba(143, 174, 145, 0.25)`. Never use default browser outlines.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 8px radius for checkboxes (softened square) and circular for radios. Inactive border in `#E3E5DC`. Active filled background in `#8FAE91` with white check/dot indicators.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tag Shape:** Full pill radius with 4px vertical by 12px horizontal padding.
- **Color Variations:**
  - *Academic / General:* Tinted Sage (`rgba(143, 174, 145, 0.18)`) background with `#343B36` text.
  - *Creative / Humanities:* Tinted Lavender (`rgba(201, 189, 224, 0.35)`) background with `#343B36` text.
  - *Discussion / Social:* Tinted Peach (`rgba(234, 198, 174, 0.35)`) background with `#343B36` text.
  - *Reminders / Highlights:* Tinted Butter (`rgba(240, 223, 167, 0.40)`) background with `#343B36` text.

### Lists & Activity Rows
- **Dividers:** 1px solid dividers using `#E3E5DC`.
- **Row Interaction:** Subtle background shift to `rgba(143, 174, 145, 0.08)` on hover with 8px radius on the hit area.

### Campus-Specific Module: Notebook Accordion
- Collapsible syllabus and note sections featuring a subtle left-hand vertical accent bar (3px width) colored according to course track using the system's secondary, tertiary, or sky-blue accents.
